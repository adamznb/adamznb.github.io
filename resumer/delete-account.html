Delete your Resumer account

You can delete your Resumer account and associated local resume data directly from the Resumer app.

Account deletion is permanent. Export or save any PDF resumes you want to keep before confirming deletion.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the Resumer app.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Open the account or profile settings.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the deletion request.

What will be deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account and account credentials.
  • Resume data stored locally by the app on the device.
  • Photos and signature images stored locally by the app.

What is not stored by Resumer

Resumer does not store resumes, photos, or signatures in Firebase Firestore or Firebase Cloud Storage. Resume content is stored locally on your device and is not synchronized to our servers.

Backups and exported files

Android device backups may retain local app data until the backup is deleted or overwritten. PDF files saved, printed, or shared from the app are stored outside the app and must be deleted separately.

If you cannot access the app

Contact us at adamzanber@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account. We will help with the account deletion request.
