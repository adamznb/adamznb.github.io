Privacy Policy for Resumer

Last updated: 30-09-2026

This Privacy Policy explains how we handles information when you use the Resumer mobile application.

Developer: Adam Zane
Contact email: adamzanber@gmail.com

1. Information We Collect

Account information

When you create or use an account, we may process:

  • Email address
  • Name or display name
  • Firebase user ID
  • Authentication provider information, such as email/password or Google Sign-In
  • Email verification and account security information

Authentication is provided by Google Firebase Authentication.

Resume information

Resumer allows you to enter and store information such as:

  • Name and job title
  • Email address and phone number
  • Address, website, and other contact details
  • Employment history
  • Education history
  • Skills, languages, interests, projects, achievements, and references
  • Resume photos
  • Signature images
  • Any other information you choose to include in a resume

Resume information is stored locally on your device using local storage. We do not store resume content in Firebase Firestore or Firebase Cloud Storage.

Photos and signatures

If you choose to add a profile photo or signature, the app accesses the image you select or capture. These images are used only to create and display your resume and generate PDF files.

The app does not access your photos, camera, or microphone unless you actively use a related feature.

Device and technical information

The app and third-party service providers may process limited technical information needed to operate authentication and the app, such as:

  • Device type and operating system
  • App version
  • Network and connectivity information
  • Security and fraud-prevention information
  • Error or diagnostic information, where provided by the operating system or service provider

We do not use the app for advertising and do not intentionally collect precise location information, contacts, calendar data, or microphone recordings.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Authenticate you securely
  • Provide resume creation and editing features
  • Store resume data locally on your device
  • Generate, print, save, or share PDF resumes
  • Respond to support or privacy requests
  • Protect the app against misuse
  • Fix technical problems and improve reliability

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Where Resume Data Is Stored

Your resume data is stored locally on your device. It is not automatically uploaded or synchronized to our servers.

As a result:

  • Your resumes may not be available on another device.
  • Deleting app data or uninstalling the app may remove locally stored resumes.
  • Device backup services may potentially include locally stored app data, depending on your Android backup settings.
  • You are responsible for keeping copies of any PDF resumes you wish to retain.

4. Third-Party Services

Firebase Authentication

We use Google Firebase Authentication to provide account registration, login, Google Sign-In, email verification, password reset, and account security features.

Firebase may process authentication-related information according to Google’s privacy practices.

Google Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

Firebase Privacy Information:
https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

We do not use Firebase Firestore or Firebase Cloud Storage to store resume content.

Google Sign-In

If you use Google Sign-In, Google processes the information required to authenticate your Google account and provide sign-in functionality.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Information may be processed or shared only:

  • With Google Firebase Authentication to provide login services
  • When you choose to share or print a generated PDF using Android or another app
  • When required by law, legal process, or governmental authority
  • To protect the rights, safety, and security of users and the app

When you use the Android share function, the PDF and its contents are transferred to the application or service you select.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, including:

  • Encrypted communication with online services
  • Firebase Authentication security controls
  • Local storage protection for app data where supported
  • Access controls for account-related operations

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. You should avoid including information in a resume that you do not want stored on your device or shared through a PDF.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Local resume data

Resume data remains on your device until you:

  • Delete the resume in the app
  • Delete local app data
  • Uninstall the app
  • Use the app’s account deletion and local-data deletion feature

Account data

Your Firebase Authentication account remains active until you delete it or request deletion.

You can delete your account through the account settings in the app, where available. Account deletion removes the Firebase Authentication account and locally stored resume data from the device. Data contained in device backups may remain until the backup is deleted or overwritten.

You may also contact us at adamzanber@gmail.com to request assistance with account or privacy-related requests.

8. Children’s Privacy

Resumer is not directed toward children under 13 years of age, or the applicable minimum age in the user’s country.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

9. International Processing

Firebase and other service providers may process authentication-related information on servers located outside your country. By using the app, you understand that information may be processed internationally in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

Resume content remains stored locally on your device unless you choose to share or export it.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, we will update the “Last updated” date and may provide additional notice in the app when appropriate.

You should review this page periodically for the latest information.
